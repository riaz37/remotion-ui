Two traps PSNR cannot see

Both found by opening rendered stills after clean numbers came back. They will recur across all 72 remaining components, so they now live in docs-internal/preview-audit-rubric.md.

An exit that empties the last sample

Opacity is gone by roughly 70% of an exit window, so an exit starting at frame 88 leaves frame 108 — the 90% sample — completely blank. PSNR passes, because a blank frame differs from a full one as much as any motion does. Start exits at frame 96.

An oscillation aliased against the sample gaps

Samples sit at frames 18, 60 and 108, so the gaps are 42 and 48 frames. A 44-frame sine puts two of three stills on the same phase: healthy numbers, visually a still image. Check periods against 42 and 48, not the 120-frame window. Periods near 32–34 are safe.

# then hand halves to remotion-speclist — never two agents at once,
# they collide on lib/component-reference.ts

Scaffolding a lane first is what keeps the shared wiring files in one commit, so parallel build agents only ever touch their own component and preview files.
