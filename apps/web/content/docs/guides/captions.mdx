## Pipeline

1. **Transcribe** audio to captions JSON (Whisper, SRT import, or `@remotion/openai-whisper`)
2. **Load** captions in your composition (fetch with `useDelayRender` if remote)
3. **Group** into pages with `groupCaptionsIntoPages()` from `caption-utils`
4. **Render** with `CaptionScene` or wire into `SocialClip` / `PodcastClip`

## Caption components

| Component | Use when |
|-----------|----------|
| [CaptionScene](/docs/components/caption-scene) | Full-frame synced caption layout |
| [CaptionHighlight](/docs/components/caption-highlight) | Word-level color emphasis |
| [KaraokeCaptions](/docs/components/karaoke-captions) | Scale or underline active word |
| [CaptionBumper](/docs/components/caption-bumper) | Short insight text between scenes |

`CaptionScene` supports `mode`: `highlight`, `karaoke-scale`, or `karaoke-underline`. Flagship compositions default to `karaoke-scale` for social-ready motion.

## Using captions over real footage

These are overlays meant to sit directly on top of your video — there is no
card or background behind the text by default. Two things matter for that to
stay legible:

- **Contrast, not decoration.** `CaptionScene` defaults to `style="shadow"`:
  a drop shadow on the text plus a light bottom scrim on `lower-third`
  placement, tuned to hold up on live-action footage without putting a
  visible box in the frame. If your footage is unusually busy or
  low-contrast (screen recordings with white backgrounds, bright skies),
  switch to `style="boxed"`, which adds a solid line-hugging background —
  the same convention YouTube and Instagram use for auto-captions.
- **Safe area, not raw offsets.** Placement always resolves through the
  shared safe-area insets (`getSafeAreaPadding`), so captions never sit
  under a platform's UI chrome (TikTok's reply bar, Reels' caption zone) at
  the edges of the frame. Don't override `top`/`left` positioning manually —
  pass `placement="lower-third"` or `placement="center"` instead.

The individual primitives (`CaptionHighlight`, `KaraokeCaptions`,
`WordPopCaptions`) carry the same `shadow` legibility default when used on
their own outside of `CaptionScene`.
