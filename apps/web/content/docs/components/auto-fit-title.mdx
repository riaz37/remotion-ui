The fit is measured before anything animates, then the words land one after
another out of their own masks — a two-word title and a twelve-word title both
fill the safe area and both land the same way.

`maxFontSize` and `minFontSize` bound the fit; the subtitle scales with the
result so the pair keeps its ratio at any length.
