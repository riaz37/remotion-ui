<ComponentPage name="simulated-cursor" preview={SimulatedCursorPreview}>

Drives a pointer across percentage-based waypoints with an arrival frame each.
Every hop runs its own spring, so the cursor decelerates into a target the way
a hand does rather than gliding at a fixed rate.

Give a waypoint a `target` to ring the hit area, and a `label` to chip the
control being used. Frames listed in `clickFrames` press the pointer down and
fire a ripple.

</ComponentPage>
