The ask types itself into the composer under a caret and flies up into the thread
on send; the assistant thinks, then streams its answer word by word; and the
preview surface assembles alongside it — status moving Idle → Rendering → Ready,
wireframe blocks landing in order, then resolving into the rendered scene under a
single sheen.

`messages` drives the whole clock: user turns type and send, assistant turns
stream, and the timing is derived from the text rather than from fixed frames.
