Each line rises out of its own mask in turn, one sweep of light crosses the
headline once it is standing, and the whole card holds under a slow push so a
long title beat never sits dead still.

Newlines in `title` are honoured; otherwise lines are balanced to
`charsPerLine`. `eyebrow` adds a chip above, `meta` a small line under the
subtitle.
