The title rises out of its mask, the button assembles and its label rises out of
the button, the address types underneath a caret that clears when it completes,
the channels list, and a single ring pulses off the button.

`cta`, `url`, `handles`, `logoSrc`, `eyebrow` and `subtitle` are each optional —
omit them and the card ends on what is left.
