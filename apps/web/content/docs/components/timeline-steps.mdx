The rail draws first, then a travelling head lands on each step: the node lights,
a ring closes over the time that step is being worked, and it checks off and dims
to done as the head moves on — so the scene ends on a fully walked timeline
rather than a row of dots that faded in.

Horizontal in landscape, a left gutter rail in portrait. Add `title`/`eyebrow`
for a heading, and `speed` to compress the walk.
