land-110m.json — Natural Earth 1:110m land polygons
===================================================

What this file is
-----------------
A plain GeoJSON FeatureCollection holding a single MultiPolygon of the world's
land, in spherical coordinates (decimal degrees, lng/lat). It is used by the
`globe-points-3d` scene to decide which dots of its Fibonacci sphere sit on
land, so the continents are the real ones.

Provenance
----------
Source data: Natural Earth, "Admin 0 — countries", 1:110m small scale,
             version 4.1.0 (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/).
Redistribution: world-atlas@2.0.2 `land-110m.json`
             (https://github.com/topojson/world-atlas), which merges the
             country boundaries into a single land topology.

Derivation: the upstream TopoJSON was arc-decoded to GeoJSON and its
coordinates rounded to 1/100 of a degree (~1 km, finer than the 1:110m source
resolves). 125 polygons, holes preserved, ring winding as published.

One edit beyond that: upstream closes the Antarctic ring along the ±180 seam,
so in planar lng/lat the polygon has no southern edge and every point below
85.61°S tests as ocean — a bald cap at the pole. The ring is closed around the
pole instead ((180,-90) → (-180,-90)), which is how well-formed GeoJSON ships
Antarctica. Verified against d3.geoContains over 20,000 points: 2 disagreements,
both within 0.5° of a coastline (planar vs great-circle edges).
